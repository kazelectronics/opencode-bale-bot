# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not people.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the appropriate issue or pull request.
- Respect privacy: do not post tokens, chat IDs, logs, or screenshots that
  contain someone else's data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Encouraging or performing attacks against systems you do not own.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors.

Report a problem privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting
form at
<https://github.com/kazelectronics/opencode-bale-bot/security/advisories/new>,
or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
